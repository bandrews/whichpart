# Passives and discretes documentation audit

**Scope:** all 14 family notes; all 19 individually notable notes for crystals, LEDs, MOSFETs, diodes, bridge rectifiers, and TVS/ESD protection; the matching curated entries and task-description strings; and the requested public documentation.

**Coverage:** Every family note was read. All 19 scoped component notes and curated entries were read, and the matching description strings were located and reviewed. Family counts and examples were treated as claims tied to the stated 2026-07-24 snapshot, not independently recomputed across every catalog record. Manufacturer-level checks concentrated on high-impact examples and explicit headline claims; this was not a fresh line-by-line verification of every numeric sentence or price/stock value. Details and exact file inventory are in [luna-passives-docs.json](luna-passives-docs.json).

## Proposed changes

1. **P2 — Resolve contradictory package guidance.** `DATA-CHANGES-MANIFEST.md` still describes SOD-123FL as pin-compatible and having the same footprint as SOD-123, while the current README says the package has different mechanical and land-pattern requirements and should not be treated as a drop-in. Replace the legacy compatibility language with an explicit superseded note and link to the current warning. Evaluate SOT-23-6L separately. Nexperia's package page identifies SOD123FL as a distinct package type and supplies package-specific dimensions and footprint information: [package page](https://www.nexperia.com/packages/SOD123FL.html), [package information](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/package-information/SOD123FL.pdf).

2. **P2 — Narrow assembly-fee wording in Basic-part detail notes.** Seven in-scope notes use the broad “no assembly surcharge” phrase. Standardize those to “no feeder-loading fee for Economic PCBA at the 2026-07-24 snapshot.” The C32346 and C412437 notes already use the precise feeder-loading/Economic PCBA wording and are not correction targets. JLCPCB describes extended-component fees within Economic PCBA and separates other charges; its Preferred Extended exemption is specifically from the Economic PCBA feeder-loading fee ([pricing](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-price), [FAQ](https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-faqs)). This is a clarity improvement to avoid implying the whole order is free of surcharges or that the statement applies to every service.

3. **P2 — Qualify the SRV05-4 signal-integrity claim.** `C85364.md` says the 3.5 pF typical capacitance is “low enough not to distort USB 2.0, Ethernet,” although the same note reports a 5 pF maximum. Change this to an intended-use statement and ask designers to validate their signal-integrity and ESD budgets in their actual layout. ProTek's sheet gives capacitance limits, not a blanket assurance for every interface implementation ([datasheet](https://protekdevices.com/wp-content/uploads/datasheets/srv05_4.pdf)).

No other confirmed high-impact claim error emerged from this scoped pass. The family notes generally distinguish catalog attributes from manufacturer specifications and include useful conditions on current, temperature, load capacitance, leakage, thermal environment, or pulse waveform. The three entries above are proposed editorial corrections; the package-manifest conflict is confirmed, the fee wording is overbroad, and the SRV05-4 statement is an evidence/qualification gap rather than a demonstrated failure in a particular design.
