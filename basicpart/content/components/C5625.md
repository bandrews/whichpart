# 74HC245D,653

## What it is

An octal bus transceiver: eight buffers that pass signals in whichever direction
a control pin selects, plus an output-enable pin that disconnects all eight. It
is the same shape of part as the SN74LVC4245A level translator, but with a single
supply — it buffers rather than translates. [1]

Two everyday uses: strengthening a bus that has to drive a long ribbon cable or
several loads, and isolating a bus so that a card can be removed without loading
the backplane. [1]

## What the datasheet actually says

**Direction is a single pin for all eight bits.** There is no per-bit control. If
your bus needs some lines in each direction simultaneously, you need two devices
or a different part. [1]

**Output enable and direction are independent.** You can hold the outputs in high
impedance while changing direction, which is what you want when switching a
shared bus to avoid contention. [1]

**Input clamp diodes** allow interfacing to voltages above V<sub>CC</sub> through
a current-limiting resistor, as elsewhere in the 74HC family. [1]

## Watch out for

- **Bus contention is your responsibility.** Two drivers fighting over one line
  can exceed the 70 mA supply limit and damage both.
- **Not a level translator.** Single supply only. Use the SN74LVC4245A for
  5 V-to-3.3 V.
- **20 pins in a 300 mil body** — it is a physically large package for what it
  does.
- **All eight or none.** No per-bit direction control.
