# HT7550-1

## What it is

The 5 V version of Holtek's HT75xx CMOS low-dropout regulator family. Everything
in the HT7533-1 note applies: a genuine LDO with tens of millivolts of drop at
light load and 2.5 µA of quiescent current, in a three-terminal SOT-89
package. [1]

It is the part to reach for when you need 5 V from a 6 V to 30 V source and care
about standby current — which is exactly where a 78L05 or an L78M05 would waste
milliamps continuously. [1]

## Key specifications

| Specification | Value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Output voltage | Fixed 5.0 V, ±3 % (4.850 V to 5.150 V at 10 mA) [1] | ±3 % is fine for logic; not a reference-grade rail. |
| Output current | 100 mA minimum, 150 mA typical [1] | Higher than the 3.3 V version's 70 mA minimum. Design to the 100 mA minimum. |
| Input voltage range | Up to 30 V; absolute maximum 33 V [1] | The line-regulation test runs from 6 V to 30 V for this variant. |
| Dropout voltage | 25 mV typical, 55 mV maximum at 1 mA (defined as the input-output difference producing a 2 % output change) [1] | A light-load figure; the drop grows with current, and Holtek specifies only the 1 mA point. |
| Quiescent current | 2.5 µA typical, 4.0 µA maximum, no load [1] | The reason to choose this over a 78L05. |
| Output accuracy | ±3 % tolerance; load regulation 25 mV typical, 60 mV maximum from 1 mA to 70 mA; line regulation 0.2 %/V maximum; temperature coefficient 100 ppm/°C typical over −40 °C to +85 °C [1] | Load regulation of 60 mV worst case is about 1.2 % at 5 V — better in relative terms than the 3.3 V part. |
| Operating temperature | −40 °C to +85 °C [1] | Industrial range. |

## What the datasheet actually says

**The ratings improved between revisions.** Older copies of this datasheet
(Rev 1.50, 2006) quote 24 V maximum input and 100 mV typical dropout; the
current Rev 2.81 (December 2025) quotes 30 V and 25 mV. The catalog matches the
current revision. [1]

**The load-regulation test range differs between variants.** For the 3.3 V part
Holtek tests 1 mA to 50 mA; for this 5 V part, 1 mA to 70 mA, with the same
25 mV typical figure — so the 5 V version regulates proportionally better. [1]

**Power dissipation is 0.50 W for SOT-89** (θ<sub>JA</sub> 200 °C/W, 25 °C, no
airflow, no heatsink). Regulating 30 V down to 5 V at 20 mA is already at that
limit. The 150 mA typical current is only reachable when the input is close to
5 V. [1]

**Line regulation is specified from 6 V to 30 V** for this variant, against
4.3 V to 30 V for the 3.3 V part — the difference being the headroom each
needs. [1]

## Watch out for

- **Package dissipation governs the usable current**: 0.5 W in SOT-89.
- **The 150 mA figure is typical, not guaranteed.** 100 mA is the minimum.
- **Dropout is specified at 1 mA only.**
- **Choose the output capacitor per the datasheet.** CMOS LDOs depend on it for
  stability.
