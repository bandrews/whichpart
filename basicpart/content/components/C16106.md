# HT7550-1

## What it is

The 5 V version of Holtek's HT75xx CMOS low-dropout regulator family. Everything
in the HT7533-1 note applies: a genuine LDO with about 100 mV of drop at light
load and 2.5 µA of quiescent current, in a three-terminal SOT-89 package. [1]

It is the part to reach for when you need 5 V from a 6 V to 24 V source and care
about standby current — which is exactly where a 78L05 or an L78M05 would waste
milliamps continuously. [1]

## Key specifications

| Specification | Value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Output voltage | Fixed 5.0 V, ±3 % (4.85 V to 5.15 V at 10 mA) [1] | ±3 % is fine for logic; not a reference-grade rail. |
| Output current | 100 mA minimum, 150 mA typical [1] | Higher than the 3.3 V version, which manages 60 mA minimum. Design to the 100 mA minimum. |
| Input voltage range | Up to 24 V; absolute maximum 26 V [1] | The line-regulation test runs from 6 V to 24 V for this variant. |
| Dropout voltage | 100 mV typical at 1 mA output [1] | A light-load figure; the drop grows with current. |
| Quiescent current | 2.5 µA typical, 5 µA maximum, no load [1] | The reason to choose this over a 78L05. |
| Output accuracy | ±3 % tolerance; load regulation 60 mV typical from 1 mA to 70 mA; line regulation 0.2 %/V [1] | Load regulation of 60 mV is about 1.2 % at 5 V — better in relative terms than the 3.3 V part. |
| Operating temperature | −40 °C to +85 °C [1] | Industrial range. |

## What the datasheet actually says

**The load-regulation test range differs between variants.** For the 3.3 V part
Holtek tests 1 mA to 50 mA; for this 5 V part, 1 mA to 70 mA. The 60 mV typical
figure is the same, so the 5 V version regulates proportionally better. [1]

**Power dissipation is 250 mW for SOT-89.** Regulating 24 V down to 5 V at 13 mA
already reaches it. The 150 mA typical current is only available when the input
is close to 5 V — around 6.5 V, given the dropout. [1]

**Line regulation is specified from 6 V to 24 V** for this variant, against 4.5 V
to 24 V for the 3.3 V part — the difference being the dropout headroom each
needs. [1]

## Watch out for

- **The catalog says 30 V input; the datasheet says 24 V.** See `ISSUES.md`.
- **Package dissipation governs the usable current.** 250 mW in SOT-89.
- **The 150 mA figure is typical, not guaranteed.** 100 mA is the minimum.
- **Choose the output capacitor per the datasheet.** CMOS LDOs depend on it for
  stability.
