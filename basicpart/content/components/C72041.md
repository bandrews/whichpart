# Blue LED (C72041)

## What it is

A blue indicator LED in the 0603 package: Everlight's 19-217/BHC-ZL1M2RY/3T,
blue with a water-clear lens. Blue is the colour the human eye is *least*
sensitive to among the common indicator colours, so a blue LED needs more
measured intensity than a green one to look equally bright — and blue indicators
have a reputation for being dazzling at night because designers overcompensate. [1]

Blue LEDs are indium gallium nitride parts, which puts the forward voltage in the
3 V region rather than the 2 V of a red LED — the same supply-headroom constraint
described in the note for the white LED (C2290). [1]

## Key specifications

| Specification | Value | Why it matters |

| Forward voltage | 2.50 V to 3.10 V at 5 mA, sorted into three bins: 2.50–2.70 V, 2.70–2.90 V and 2.90–3.10 V, each ±0.1 V [1] | On a 3.3 V rail the headroom across your resistor could be 0.8 V or 0.2 V depending on which bin you receive — a four-to-one swing in LED current. 5 V drive is far more predictable. |

| Viewing angle | 120° [1] | Wide, as usual for an 0603 indicator. |

## What the datasheet actually says

**10 mA is the limit, not 20 mA.** This is the specification most likely to catch
someone out. The habit of sizing an LED resistor for 20 mA would put this part at
twice its absolute maximum forward current, and its 40 mW dissipation rating is
less than half the green 19-217's. Everlight specifies everything about this LED
at 5 mA, which is the current it is really meant to run at. [1]

**Forward voltage is binned, and the bins matter on a 3.3 V rail.** Bin 9 runs
2.50–2.70 V, bin 10 runs 2.70–2.90 V and bin 11 runs 2.90–3.10 V. A single
resistor sized for a 2.6 V part will drive a 3.0 V part far more dimly, and the
order code does not pin the bin down. [1]

**The ESD rating is only 150 V, human body model.** Very low by modern standards
— many components in this catalog withstand 2,000 V. Treat these as
static-sensitive on the bench and in assembly. [1]

**Comparisons with the green LED need care.** This part's intensity is specified
at 5 mA; the green 19-217 (C72043) is specified at 20 mA. The bare numbers —
28.5 against 285 mcd — are not measured under the same conditions. [1]

**Availability was a problem when last checked.** The LCSC listing showed "not
available now" on 2026-08-19, with a 20-piece minimum order when stocked.
Combined with its ordinary-Extended assembly status, this is a recommendation
that may not be orderable at all — see `ISSUES.md`. [2]

## Watch out for

- **10 mA absolute maximum.** Do not reuse a 20 mA resistor value. [1]
- **Marginal on 3.3 V**, and the forward-voltage bins make it unpredictable
  there. Drive from 5 V or a current source. [1]
- **Check availability before designing it in** — the listing was marked
  unavailable when checked. [2]
- **150 V ESD rating.** Handle accordingly. [1]
- **Extended-tier assembly costs more** than the Basic-tier red (C2286) and
  white (C2290) LEDs beside it in the picks list.
- **Always fit a series resistor**, and check the cathode marking against
  Everlight's package drawing — 0603 polarity marks are not standardised. [1]
