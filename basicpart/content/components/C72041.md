## What it is

A blue indicator LED in the 0603 package, recommended alongside the red and white
LEDs on the site's "Our Picks" page. Blue is the colour the human eye is *least*
sensitive to among the common indicator colours, so a blue LED needs more
measured intensity than a green one to look equally bright — and blue indicators
have a reputation for being dazzling at night because designers overcompensate. [1]

Blue LEDs use indium gallium nitride, which puts their forward voltage in the 3 V
region rather than the 2 V of a red LED — the same constraint described in the
note for the white LED (C2290). [1]

## What can be said

**It is an ordinary Extended part at JLCPCB.** That is the substantive fact in
the repository, and it has a real cost consequence: Extended parts attract an
assembly surcharge and may require a dedicated feeder, so a design using this
LED costs more to assemble than one using the Basic-tier red (C2286) or white
(C2290) LEDs already in the catalog.

**A Basic-tier blue LED may well exist.** Since the red and white indicators in
this catalog are Basic parts from Hubei KENTO, it is worth checking whether the
same manufacturer's blue part qualifies before accepting the surcharge.

## Watch out for

- **No specification data is available here.** Do not design a resistor value
  from this page; get the actual part's datasheet from LCSC first.
- **Extended-tier assembly costs more.** Check the surcharge before committing.
- **Blue LEDs are not interchangeable with red ones electrically.** The forward
  voltage is roughly a volt higher, so a resistor sized for red will run a green
  LED very dim or not at all.
