# AMS1117-5.0

## What it is

The 5 V version of the AMS1117. Same package, same 1 A rating, same dropout
behaviour as the 3.3 V part — the difference is where the input has to sit. This
one is specified for inputs from 6.5 V to 12 V. [1]

That makes it a straightforward way to get 5 V from a 9 V battery, a 12 V supply,
or an unregulated wall adapter. It is not a way to get 5 V from a 6 V source with
any margin. [1]

## Key specifications

| Specification | Value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Output voltage | Fixed 5.0 V, specified as 4.900 V to 5.100 V (±2 %) for 6.5 V ≤ V<sub>IN</sub> ≤ 12 V and 10 mA ≤ I<sub>OUT</sub> ≤ 1 A [1] | The 6.5 V floor is the dropout showing up in the specification. |
| Output current | 1 A; current limit 1.1 A minimum, 1.5 A typical at V<sub>IN</sub> − V<sub>OUT</sub> = 2 V [1] | Thermally limited in practice — see below. |
| Input voltage range | 6.5 V to 12 V for guaranteed 5 V regulation; the catalog records a 15 V supply figure [1] [2] | Above 12 V the datasheet no longer guarantees the output specification. |
| Dropout voltage | 1.1 V typical, 1.25 V maximum at 1 A [1] | The catalog records 1.3 V for this variant. Either way, over a volt. |
| Quiescent current | Not stated as a headline; the catalog records 5 mA [1] [2] | Not a low-power part. |
| Output accuracy | ±2 %; load regulation 0.2 % typical, 1 % maximum [1] | Fine for logic and most peripherals. |
| Operating temperature | Operating junction temperature −20 °C to +125 °C [1] | Note the −20 °C floor — see `ISSUES.md`. |

## What the datasheet actually says

**Thermal dissipation is worse here than on the 3.3 V part**, because the input
has to be higher. Regulating 12 V to 5 V at 500 mA dissipates 3.5 W — far beyond
what a SOT-223 will shed. For anything above a couple of hundred milliamps from a
12 V rail, a switching regulator such as the XL1509-5.0 (C61063) is the right
answer, and it is in this catalog too.

**Quiescent current flows into the load.** AMS makes the point that unlike PNP
regulators, which waste up to 10 % of the output current as quiescent current,
the AMS1117's flows to the load and so improves efficiency. [1]

**The 4 cm output-capacitor rule applies here too.** It is a front-page note on
the shared datasheet. [1]

## Watch out for

- **6.5 V minimum input.** A 6 V supply will not do.
- **Heat.** From 12 V this part is a heater as much as a regulator.
- **On-chip thermal limiting will fold the output back** rather than fail —
  which looks like a mysteriously low 5 V rail rather than an obvious fault.
- **Keep the output capacitor within 4 cm.**
