# LM358DR2G

## What it is

The LM358 is half an LM324: two general-purpose op-amps in an 8-pin package, with
the same defining property — a single-supply part whose input range extends down
to the negative rail, so you can amplify ground-referenced signals without a
negative supply. onsemi describes it as "equivalent to one-half of an
LM324". [1]

At under six cents in ones, it is the cheapest op-amp in this catalog and one of
the cheapest ICs of any kind. Use it wherever "good enough" analog is what the
job needs. [2]

## Key specifications

| Specification | Value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Function | Two independent operational amplifiers, internally compensated, with short-circuit protected outputs and ESD clamps on the inputs [1] | Two amplifiers, no external compensation, and a true differential input stage. |
| Supply voltage | 3.0 V to 32 V single supply, or ±1.0 V to ±16 V split (absolute maximum ±16 V or 32 V) [1] | The very wide single-supply range is why it is still everywhere. |
| Input offset voltage | 2.0 mV typical, 7.0 mV maximum for the LM358 grade at 25 °C, over V<sub>CC</sub> = 5 V to 30 V [1] | Loose but predictable. Not a precision part. |
| Gain bandwidth | Not stated in the electrical characteristics table of this document; the LM358 family is conventionally about 1 MHz [1] | Slow. Fine for sensors and control loops, not for signals. |
| Output swing | Output voltage range includes the negative supply; output current 40 mA per the catalog record [1] [2] | The output goes to ground but not to the positive rail. |
| Supply current | 0.7 mA typical, 1.2 mA maximum for the whole device at V<sub>CC</sub> = 5 V; 1.5 mA typical at 30 V [1] | About 350 µA per amplifier. Genuinely low power. |
| Operating temperature | 0 °C to +70 °C for the LM358, which is this part. (The LM2904 covers −40 °C to +105 °C, the NCV2904 −40 °C to +125 °C) [1] | **Commercial grade.** The LM2904DR2G is the industrial version in the same package and at almost the same price. |

## What the datasheet actually says

**The input range is specified as 0 V to V<sub>CC</sub> − 1.7 V**, wider at the
bottom than at the top. Everything about this part is designed around
ground-referenced single-supply operation. [1]

**Channel separation is −120 dB typical** from 1 kHz to 20 kHz — good isolation
between the two amplifiers. [1]

**Common-mode rejection is 70 dB minimum, 85 dB typical** with a source
resistance of 10 kΩ or less. [1]

**ESD clamps on the inputs "increase ruggedness of the device without affecting
operation"** — onsemi calls this out as a feature, which matters for a part often
wired to the outside world. [1]

**The datasheet covers nine part numbers.** LM358, LM358A and LM358E differ in
offset-voltage grade; LM2904 variants differ in temperature range. The columns in
the specification tables are not interchangeable. [1]

## Watch out for

- **0 °C to +70 °C.** For a few hundredths of a cent more the LM2904DR2G gives
  you −40 °C to +105 °C in the same package.
- **The output does not reach the positive rail.** Same trap as the LM324.
- **Crossover distortion near zero output** is the LM358's known weakness; the
  usual fix is a resistor pulling the output toward the negative rail.
- **Not a precision or audio part.** For DC accuracy use the OP07C; for audio
  the NE5532 or TL072.
