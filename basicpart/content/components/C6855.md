# SP485EEN-L/TR

## What it is

The SP485E is a 5 V half-duplex RS-485 transceiver whose distinguishing feature
is ESD protection: ±15 kV on the bus pins, tested to both the Human Body Model
and IEC 61000-4-2. That matters because RS-485 buses run between buildings and
machines, on cable that people plug and unplug. [1]

Functionally it is the same shape of part as the SN75176B — one driver, one
receiver, separate enables, industry-standard pinout — but with the ESD hardening
and lower power consumption of MaxLinear's BiCMOS process. [1]

## Key specifications

| Specification | Value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Function | Half-duplex RS-485/RS-422 transceiver, one driver and one receiver, with separate enables for multi-drop configurations [1] | Half duplex on two wires. |
| Signalling standard | RS-485 and RS-422 [1] | Interoperates with any compliant device. |
| Maximum data rate | Up to 10 Mbps under load [1] | Well beyond what most industrial links use. |
| Supply voltage | 5 V only [1] | A 5 V part. For 3.3 V use the SP3485EN (C8963), also in this catalog. |
| Isolation or protection | ±15 kV Human Body Model, ±15 kV IEC 61000-4-2 air discharge, ±8 kV IEC 61000-4-2 contact discharge, on the bus pins [1] | The IEC figures are the ones that matter for a real product — HBM is a manufacturing test, IEC is a field-use test. |
| Operating temperature | −40 °C to +85 °C per the catalog record [2] | Industrial range. |

## What the datasheet actually says

**Supply current is 900 µA with the driver enabled and 600 µA with it
disabled**, both with no load. That is low for a 5 V transceiver, and it is what
"low power BiCMOS" buys you. [1]

**The shutdown mode belongs to the SP481E, not this part.** MaxLinear's datasheet
covers both devices; the low-power shutdown feature is listed specifically for
the SP481E. The SP485E has no shutdown pin. [1]

**Pin-compatible with the SP481 and SP485** and with the industry standard, so it
substitutes directly into existing layouts. [1]

**Three ESD numbers, not one.** ±15 kV HBM, ±15 kV IEC air discharge, ±8 kV IEC
contact discharge. Quoting only the largest is common and misleading — the
contact-discharge figure is the harshest test and the most relevant to a
connector someone can touch. [1]

## Watch out for

- **5 V only.** A 3.3 V microcontroller needs level shifting on the logic side,
  or use the SP3485EN instead.
- **ESD protection is on the bus pins**, not on the logic pins.
- **Termination and fail-safe biasing are external**, as with any RS-485
  transceiver.
- **Read the right column.** The datasheet covers the SP481E and SP485E together
  and they differ in shutdown capability.
