# Green LED (C72043)

## What it is

A green indicator LED in the 0603 package: Everlight's 19-217/GHC-YR1S2/3T,
brilliant green with a water-clear lens. Green is the colour the human eye is
most sensitive to, so a green indicator looks brighter than a red or blue one of
the same measured intensity. [1]

Like most modern green LEDs it is an indium gallium nitride part, which puts its
forward voltage in the 3 V region rather than the 2 V of a red LED — the same
supply-headroom constraint described in the note for the white LED (C2290). [1]

## Key specifications

| Specification | Value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Colour | Brilliant green, dominant wavelength 520–535 nm, peak wavelength 525 nm typical, spectral width 35 nm, water-clear lens [1] | The "pure green" region — noticeably bluer and more vivid than the yellowish-green of older LED chemistry. |
| Forward voltage | 2.7 V minimum, 3.3 V typical, 3.7 V maximum, measured at 5 mA [1] | **Higher than a 3.3 V rail can drive.** Even the typical figure equals the rail exactly, and the worst case is 3.7 V. This LED effectively requires a 5 V supply or a constant-current driver. |
| Forward current | 25 mA absolute maximum continuous; 100 mA peak at 1/10 duty and 1 kHz. Power dissipation 95 mW [1] | These are limits, not targets — a few milliamps is plenty for an indicator, and running near the maximum shortens life. |
| Luminous intensity | 112 mcd to 285 mcd at 20 mA, ±11 % tolerance [1] | Everlight sorts parts into bins R1 (112–140), R2 (140–180), S1 (180–225) and S2 (225–285 mcd). The `R1S2` in the order code spans the whole range, so a reel can contain any of them — a 2.5:1 brightness spread between units. |
| Viewing angle | 120° [1] | Wide, as usual for an 0603 indicator. |
| Operating temperature | −40 °C to +85 °C. Storage −40 °C to +90 °C [1] | Industrial range. |

## What the datasheet actually says

**The part number encodes the bins.** In `19-217/GHC-YR1S2/3T`, the `Y` is the
dominant-wavelength bin (525–530 nm) and `R1`…`S2` are the lowest and highest
luminous-intensity bins. Because that span covers every bin Everlight makes for
this device, you are not buying a narrow brightness match — expect visible
variation between units on the same board. [1]

**The ESD rating is only 150 V, human body model.** That is very low by modern
standards, and low enough that ordinary handling precautions matter: many
components in this catalog withstand 2,000 V. Treat these as static-sensitive on
the bench and in assembly. [1]

**Comparisons with the blue LED need care.** This part's intensity is specified
at 20 mA; the blue 19-217 (C72041) is specified at 5 mA. The bare numbers — 285
against 28.5 mcd — are not measured under the same conditions. [1]

**Availability was a problem when last checked.** The LCSC listing showed "not
available now" on 2026-08-19, with a 50-piece minimum order when stocked.
Combined with its ordinary-Extended assembly status, this is a recommendation
that may not be orderable at all — see `ISSUES.md`. [2]

## Watch out for

- **Not drivable from 3.3 V.** Plan for 5 V or a current source. [1]
- **Check availability before designing it in** — the listing was marked
  unavailable when checked. [2]
- **150 V ESD rating.** Handle accordingly. [1]
- **Extended-tier assembly costs more** than the Basic-tier red (C2286) and
  white (C2290) LEDs beside it in the picks list.
- **Always fit a series resistor**, and check the cathode marking against
  Everlight's package drawing — 0603 polarity marks are not standardised. [1]
